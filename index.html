💙 A Letter For You

Dear Ahmer,

Happy 18th Birthday, my favorite person. 💙

It's funny how life works.

We met through a group chat, a place where I never expected to find someone who would become so important to me.

At that time, I wasn't interested in talking to anyone, and I definitely wasn't looking for love.

But then... there was you.

I still don't know how you managed to do it. Maybe it was your gentle way of texting, maybe it was your kindness, or maybe it was the love and care you showed me from the very beginning.

Whatever it was, it slowly found its way into my heart.

I didn't fall in love with you all at once.

I fell in love little by little.

With the way you take care of me.

With the way you make me feel seen, heard, and valued.

With the way you always put in effort for us.

With your kindness.

With the way you make me believe that I matter.

You have no idea how much the little things mean to me.

Your voice has become one of my favorite sounds.

Your eyes always make me smile.

Your random little "I love you" texts never fail to make my day.

Whenever I get angry, you calm me down instead of making things worse.

Whenever I start overthinking, you're always there to reassure me that everything will be okay.

And every single time you call me "babieee"... My heart melts all over again.

Some of my happiest memories aren't grand moments.

They're the simple ones.

Listening to your voice notes over and over.

Spending my time on calls with you.

Playing Ludo together and laughing over the silliest things.

Today, you're turning eighteen.

A brand-new chapter of your life begins.

And on this special day, I pray that Allah blesses you with endless happiness, good health, peace, success, and everything your heart desires.

I pray that He guides you, protects you, and grants you a future brighter than you could ever imagine.

And selfishly...

I pray that our love continues to grow stronger with every passing day, and that one day, In shaa Allah, we celebrate your birthday together instead of through a screen.

Thank you for loving me.

Thank you for choosing me.

Thank you for making me feel safe, appreciated, and loved.

I hope you never forget how incredibly special you are not just today, but every single day.

Happy 18th Birthday, my birthday boy. 💙

I'll always be cheering for you, believing in you, and praying for you.

Forever yours,
Muntaha ❤️
